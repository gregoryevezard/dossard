Privacy Policy

Aide (FR) Confidentialité (FR) Privacy Privacidad (ES)

Dossard collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no remote database, no usage analytics, no advertising and no tracker. Your races, your notes and your images stay on your iPhone.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who publishes this app

Dossard is developed and published by an individual, Grégory Evezard. There is no company behind it, no investor, and no business model built on your data.

What Dossard stores, and where

Everything you enter or import — races, dates, places, distances, courses, participation status, private notes, images — is stored only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone.

This data is never sent to the developer or to any third party. There is no Dossard server it could go to. The app does not sync it through iCloud.

If you have iPhone backups enabled, this data may be included in your iCloud or local backup, like any other app’s. That backup is governed by your Apple account and Apple’s terms, not by Dossard.

Network connections, and what triggers them

Dossard never connects to anything on its own initiative. Every connection below follows an action you just took.

1. The race website you provide

When you share a link to Dossard or ask it to read a page, your iPhone downloads that page directly from the site itself, with no intermediary. Parsing then happens on the device.

The connection uses no cookies, no cache and no stored credentials. The site sees a request coming from your internet connection, as if you had opened it in a browser.

2. Pages that site links to, when essential information is missing

If the main page lacks an essential detail, Dossard may open a page that the same site points to — typically “Course” or “Registration”. Those visits honour the site’s robots.txt, happen only during an import, are named in the progress indicator, are recorded in the race’s provenance, and can be switched off in Settings.

3. OpenStreetMap, to locate a town

To place a race on the map, Dossard queries the free public Nominatim service from OpenStreetMap (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), only when you ask for it, never automatically. Only the race’s place name is sent — never your location, never any identifier relating to you.

Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

4. Gemini web search — optional, off by default

Dossard can look up a race from a plain description. This feature uses Google’s Gemini API and works only if you supply your own API key. Without a key it is absent, and the app remains fully usable.

  • Your key is stored in your iPhone’s Keychain. It is never stored in the clear, never exported, never written to a log, and is sent only to Google, in a request header.
  • Only the search text you write is sent to Google. Not your notebook, not your notes, not your location.
  • What Google does with that request is governed by its own terms, which you accept when you create your key.
  • You can delete the key at any time in Settings; the feature disappears immediately.

Your location

Dossard requests location access only for the Map screen, and only while that screen is showing. It reads your position at kilometre accuracy, once per appearance.

That position is used solely to compute, on the device, the distance between you and a race. It is never stored, never exported and never transmitted to anyone.

If you decline the permission, the map, its pins and its links keep working. Only the distance line disappears.

Your photos and images

When you import a poster or a screenshot, text recognition runs on the device, using Apple’s Vision framework. The image is not sent anywhere.

Before being stored, the image is downsampled and its metadata — including any GPS coordinates your camera may have written into it — is stripped. The recognised text is not kept in full.

Apple Intelligence

Where available and when you enable it, Dossard may use Apple’s models running on your iPhone to help complete a hard-to-read race card. That processing does not leave the device. Dossard does not use Private Cloud Compute and has no remote model fallback on this path.

The feature is optional: without Apple Intelligence, creating, importing, browsing and sharing all work normally.

Sharing between people

Dossard shares races as files (.racecard, .racelist) that you send yourself through Messages, AirDrop, Mail or Files. There is no automatic sync between users and no exchange server.

Your private notes are never included in a shared file. Your participation intent is included only if you explicitly choose to.

Children

Dossard is not directed at children, and collects no data regardless of who uses it.

Your rights, and how to exercise them

The GDPR, Québec’s Law 25 and equivalent laws give you rights of access, correction, erasure and portability over your personal data. Those rights are normally exercised against whoever holds your data.

Here, that is you. Since no data reaches the developer, there is nobody to ask:

  • Access your data: it is visible in the app.
  • Correct it: every race card is editable.
  • Export it: the share feature produces documented, readable files, with no server involved.
  • Erase it: delete a race in the app, or uninstall Dossard — uninstalling removes all of the app’s data from your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Any change that introduced data collection would be announced in the release notes before taking effect.
